# Per-publication publisher authorship audit — 23 October 2026

All 94 scholarly records were inventoried individually. Publisher/original publication destinations were attempted for 39 international journals, 5 Chinese publications and the one conference article with a DOI. The other 47 conference records and 2 theses have no identified publisher article page; their prior source/manual records remain unchanged.

34 of 39 international-journal records have their author order, printed affiliation links and corresponding-author designation checked against original published PDFs or publisher-deposited full-text JATS. Two Materialsnet articles additionally have author/affiliation links checked on their original pages; three Chinese pages confirm names/order only. Five international journals and the DOI-bearing conference paper remain without sufficient accessible publisher evidence for affiliation/role verification. This is not a claim that every publisher web page was readable.

ScienceDirect required a human CAPTCHA; Wiley required security verification; ACS/RSC returned HTTP 403, and MDPI returned a verification interstitial. These were not bypassed. Elsevier linkinghub redirect pages were followed and were not counted as author evidence. Public copies of the actual publisher originals were obtained from the authors’ Frontier Materials Research Group site and the National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University repository. Every used PDF was checked for its article DOI, avoiding a similarly linked but incorrect ChemComm PDF. PMC/Europe PMC evidence is publisher-deposited article text, not a live publisher-page visit.

Scope: publication-specific names/order, affiliation links, correspondence and explicit equal-contribution statements. Existing ORCID/OpenAlex identities and current global author positions were not independently reverified. Lack of an explicit equal-contribution statement stays unknown. Printed historical departments/addresses and source spelling are retained; affiliation numbering may be assigned for publisher originals whose author groups have no printed numbers.

## Data and rendering ownership

`data/publications.json` owns the publication authors and units. `sync_publication_authors.py` adds a missing name as a pending global profile without inferring current employment. `app.js` and `build_seo.py` render the same verified original affiliation text without repeating department/institution/address. Static publication cards, per-article HTML, structured author data and copyable citations are regenerated through the existing SEO generator. No citation counts, analytics inputs, patent data or workflows are changed.
